# Sales-Dashboard
1. Project Title 
Madhav Ecommerce Sales & Profit Performance Analytics Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard to track overall e-commerce sales, profitability, product category trends, and customer payment behavior.

2. Short Description 
The Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard is designed to provide actionable business insights into e-commerce operations. It helps business owners and data analysts track key revenue drivers, identify top-performing states and customers, analyze monthly profit fluctuations, and understand customer purchasing preferences across payment modes.

3. Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
 * Power BI Desktop: Core platform used for designing the dashboard layout, visualizations, and custom color themes.
 * Power Query: Utilized for data cleaning, transforming order and customer datasets, and shaping column formats.
 * DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Used to calculate key performance indicators (KPIs) such as Total Amount (438K), Total Profit (37K), Total Quantity (6K), and Average Order Value (AOV - 121K).
 * Data Modeling: Created relationships between sales transactions, categories, and geographical tables to enable multi-dimensional filtering (by State and Quarter).

4. Features

Business Problem & Goal
E-commerce businesses need to quickly identify which regions, categories, and payment methods contribute most to profit, while monitoring monthly performance trends to address losses (e.g., in months like May).
Walkthrough of Key Visuals
 * KPI Cards: Instant summary of core metrics including Total Amount (438K), Sum of Profit (37K), Sum of Quantity (6K), and Sum of AOV (121K).
 * Sales by State (Bar Chart): Highlights top revenue-generating states such as Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Uttar Pradesh.
 * Quantity by Category (Donut Chart): Breaks down sold units across product categories — Clothing (63%), Electronics (21%), and Furniture (17%).
 * Profit by Month (Column Chart): Tracks monthly profitability throughout the year, highlighting peak profit months (January, November) and loss periods (May).
 * Quantity by Payment Mode (Donut Chart): Analyzes customer payment preferences — COD (43.74%), UPI (20.61%), Debit Card (13.2%), Credit Card (11.97%), and EMI (10.49%).
 * Profit by Sub-Category (Bar Chart): Pinpoints top profit-yielding sub-categories like Printers, Bookcases, Saree, Accessories, and Tables.
 * Interactive Slicers: Allows seamless filtering by quarters (Qtr 1 to Qtr 4) and individual Indian states (e.g., Delhi, Bihar, Goa, Gujarat).
Business Impact & Insights
 * Primary Revenue Driver: Clothing dominates unit sales volume at 63%.
 * Preferred Payment Method: Cash on Delivery (COD) accounts for nearly 44% of total orders, showing a strong consumer preference for COD.
 * Geographic Focus: High performance in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh indicates strong regional sales penetration.
